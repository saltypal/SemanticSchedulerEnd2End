The missing diagnostics are now generated by diagnostics.py: grouped training-size curves, internal MLP epoch loss/accuracy and original96-row validation confusion matrix. Curves use ONLY the204 training rows. Three fixed grouped folds and three subset-order seeds are descriptive repetitions, not nine independent datasets. ## Inspected primary literature
- MEDIC original paper https://mimran.me/papers/MEDIC_a_multi-task_dataset_for_disaster_image_classification_2022.pdf and official code https://github.com/firojalam/medic compare transfer-learning CNNs including ResNet18 and EfficientNet-B1. Its shared-backbone multi-task heads address disaster/informativeness/humanitarian/damage labels. Published weightedF1 is not our macroF1; none is our four-class urgency target.
- CrisisViT https://arxiv.org/html/2401.02838v1 and https://github.com/longkukuhi/CrisisViT use ViT-Base plus Incidents1M domain pretraining. We inspected the architecture but did not download/validate its domain checkpoints. Its mechanism supports testing adapted representation, not automatic superiority for our task.
- EfficientNet-B1 torchvision https://docs.pytorch.org/vision/main/models/generated/torchvision.models.efficientnet_b1.html uses ImageNet pretrained weights. We choose explicit IMAGENET1K_V2, a new four-logit head, three head-only warmup epochs and partial unfreezing thereafter.
