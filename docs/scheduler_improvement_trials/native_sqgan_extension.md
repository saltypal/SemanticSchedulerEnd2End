# SQ-GAN reader qualification and 24-image replay

## Decision

The native codec and controlled replay run end to end. The current frozen road-damage reader failed the development qualification after native decoding. The header cue remains experimental and the protected holdout stays closed.

## Ordered checklist

1. **Native measurements complete.** Extended the initial six-image pilot with 18 image-disjoint LADI development examples. All 18 encoded and decoded with the pinned SQ-GAN checkpoint and no codec failures. Median preparation was 6,010 ms, decode 16,443 ms and reader 559 ms on the local CPU. Each payload was 464 bytes.
2. **Simulator verified.** The original six scenarios reproduced with identical metrics and timelines after adding the receipt merge path. The expanded replay regenerated byte-for-byte. It uses per-image measured service, independent synthetic sender queues, synthetic captures and ideal delivery. Every policy sees the same inputs and deadlines. An overload stress control repeated the 24 jobs four times. At nominal load 1.2, every policy left one decode unfinished and 12 jobs unstarted with and without bursts. Failure handling remains covered by the existing fixtures.
3. **Cue measured, predictive qualification failed.** Seventeen of 18 new images had a defined cue. Its median extra sender work was 1.17 ms and its encoding uses one byte. The grouped comparison could not fit a model because there were no successful positive reader outcomes. A completed cue extraction is not evidence of predictive lift.
4. **Policy runs completed, efficacy unestablished.** FIFO, SPI-first and SPI-first plus the cue ran at 0.5, 0.9 and 1.2 nominal load, with and without bursts. All produced zero timely correct positives. In the 24-job bursty overload trace, p95 observed wait was 96.63 s for FIFO, 227.70 s for SPI-first and 245.01 s with the cue. No independent Critical references were available, so no Critical-preservation claim is made.
5. **Holdout decision complete for this configuration.** No-go. A final evaluation remains gated on a qualified reader and independently labeled multi-event cohort. No protected holdout labels or outcomes were opened and no scheduler was retrained.

## Locked reader comparison

The 18-image extension contains nine visible road-damage positives and nine negatives. The alternative threshold was fixed from the original six images before inspecting extension results. It was set just above the maximum reconstructed-image score among calibration negatives (0.0137618). The pilot gate required at least two-thirds recall and at most one false positive.

| Extension input | Threshold | True positives | False positives | Average precision |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Original source | 0.5 | 2/9 | 0/9 | 0.868 |
| Resized input | 0.5 | 1/9 | 0/9 | 0.834 |
| SQ-GAN reconstruction | 0.5 | 0/9 | 0/9 | 0.574 |
| SQ-GAN reconstruction | Locked lower threshold | 0/9 | 0/9 | 0.574 |

The groups are image-disjoint and no raw hash crosses the six/18 split. Event and near-duplicate separation were not established for that split. The publisher-source audit independently matched all 24 annotation vectors by validation row and checked publisher originals against cached thumbnails. Six rows map to three named events and 18 have numeric source prefixes whose disaster identity is unresolved. Only matched named events enter the grouped diagnostic. Native labels are image-level visible damage, separate from Critical urgency. The publisher's validation cohort is described in the [LADI dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/MITLL/LADI-v2-dataset).

## Higher-retention control

Reran the same six calibration images with the image latent fraction increased from 0.25 to 0.50. The semantic fraction stayed at 0.25. Source hashes and manifest hash match the first pilot. All six jobs succeeded. Payloads grew from 464 to 624 B. Both settings detected 0/3 positives after reconstruction at the frozen 0.5 cutoff, with 0/3 false positives. This comparison provides no reason to increase the default rate for this reader. It does not select a new rate or threshold. The higher-retention receipt is [saved here](native_sqgan_half_retention_pilot.json).

Verification includes 27 focused tests, exact matched arrivals, deadlines and payload identities across policies, and byte-for-byte reproduction of the 24-image replay. The next experiment should qualify a stronger frozen reader on fresh source images before testing it on paired SQ-GAN reconstructions.
