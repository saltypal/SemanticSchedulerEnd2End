## JPEG control and reader failure

The same balanced development validation sample contains 12 native visible-road-damage positives and 12 negatives. At a fixed 0.5 cutoff, an initial direct 512 × 256 resize followed by JPEG reconstruction detected zero positives. The revised pilot preserves aspect ratio, pads to native codec size and removes padding before reading. Comparing stages on those same images gives the following results.

| Reader input | True positives | False positives |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Original image | 3/12 | 0/12 |
| Resized content, before codec | 2/12 | 0/12 |
| Reconstructed content | 0/12 | 0/12 |

This controls preprocessing and shows an additional loss after the tested JPEG encoding. It is a small development diagnostic of the frozen reader. It does not estimate SQ-GAN task quality or establish a new reader threshold. Median sender preparation was 27.31 ms, JPEG decode 1.08 ms, task reader 565.77 ms and payload 15,234.5 B on the local CPU. The source and resized reader calls are auxiliary diagnostics and excluded from the modeled sender service.

The staged replay used four independent serial sender queues and a single nonpreemptive receiver with decode plus reader service. Deadlines are six median service times after capture. Nominal offered load settings were 0.5, 0.9 and 1.2 with and without bursts. Each policy sees identical offered jobs. At 1.2, the arrival-conditional p95 observed wait was 2,226 ms without a burst and 3,213 ms with a burst. Zero correct positives were reported in these runs because the frozen reconstructed-image reader emitted no positives. The cohort has no independent Critical reference or SPI values. The policy equality is a fallback check. The replay regenerated byte-for-byte from the saved measurements.

## Cue and verification

The SQ01 image bitmap identifies the selected 16 × 16 latent positions. The prototype measures the fraction of teacher-road pixel mass covered by those positions, excluding pad pixels. It encodes 0 to 1 into values 0 to 254 and reserves 255 for unavailable. No fresh segmentation pass is required for the cue itself because the codec already needs the map. The native pilot records the additional time to parse the selected positions and compute the cue. A ready policy receives only routing identity, arrival time, SPI and the quantized cue. A failed decode or missing completion earns no task credit. The cue is eligible only within the best SPI tier. No codec or ranker retraining was performed.

Twenty-four focused tests passed. The historical no-COCO header and scheduler implementations retain their existing paths.
