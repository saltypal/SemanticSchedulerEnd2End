## Further splits after the mechanical move

- `SemParam/runtime.py` now owns image-only execution; the former combined showcase lives in `INFRA/experiments/semantic_showcase.py`.
- Swin feature capture moved from `CodebookParam/swin_representation.py` to `EncDec/Models/SwinJSCC/features.py`; SemParam consumes `INFRA.features.FeatureProvider`.
- `scripts/train_parameter_codebook.py` delegates to `SemParam/training.py`.
- Active semantic settings moved to `SemParam/config/extraction.yaml` and `SemParam/config/codebook_training.yaml`; old `config/stage*.yaml` remain for compatibility.
- `E2EMain.py` dispatches to `INFRA/cli.py`; old stage actions delegate to `INFRA/legacy_cli.py`.
- Historical notebook mapping is in `docs/NOTEBOOK_MIGRATION.md`.
